Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Reviewwo QR ("we", "our", or "us") provides a software-as-a-service platform helping local businesses collect customer reviews and manage their Google Business Profile reputation. We are firmly committed to safeguarding user data and privacy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect business details (name, email, phone number, Google listing IDs) provided during registration. For end-customers scanning QR codes, we do not require accounts, phone numbers, or passwords unless they choose to submit private feedback through the negative review shield.

3. How We Use Data

Data collected is used strictly to deliver the review acceleration service, generate AI draft options, power 24/7 autopilot replies, and provide aggregate scan analytics to the business subscriber. We never sell customer or business data to third parties.

4. Google API Services Compliance

Reviewwo QR adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, ensuring all tokens and permissions utilized for Google Business Profile review automation are encrypted and used solely for user-authorized actions.

5. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding data privacy or to request data deletion, contact us at support@reviewwoqr.com.
